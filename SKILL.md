## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a selective memory, not a copy of the photograph. Keep only the most recognizable silhouette, structure and relation. Rebuild them as a quiet folk-modern illustration: rounded simplified forms, flat perspective, a loose imperfect single line, powdery gouache, crayon grain and a little Risograph unevenness on warm paper. The subject stays small and may sit off-center, cropped, or floating in a large field of untouched paper. Take the most living colors from the source and hold them to a soft limited palette. It is not a filled scene, a smooth vector, a plastic render, a fluorescent candy palette, or a commercial illustration.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
